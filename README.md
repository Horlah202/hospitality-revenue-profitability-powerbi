# Hospitality Revenue & Profitability Analysis

## Overview

This project analyzes six months of hospitality operational data
from a business operating hotel rooms and short-stay apartments.

The objective was to identify revenue drivers, potential revenue
leakage, cost pressures, pricing opportunities and profitability
trends.

## Business Questions

1. What drives revenue?
2. Where is money being lost?
3. How can pricing and occupancy be improved?

## Key Results

| KPI | Result |
|---|---:|
| Completed Revenue | ₦53.55M |
| Total Expenses | ₦31.98M |
| Operating Profit | ₦21.57M |
| Profit Margin | 40.3% |
| ADR | ~₦42.4K |
| Completed Room Nights | 1,263 |
| Cancellation Rate | 16.25% |
| No-Show Rate | 5.25% |
| Unrealized Booking Value | ₦13.08M |

## Key Insights

- Executive rooms generated the highest completed-booking revenue.
- Power and Internet were the largest expense categories.
- Cancellation and no-show bookings represented significant
  unrealized booking value.
- ADR varied across months and booking channels.

## Data Limitation

True occupancy could not be calculated because room inventory
was not provided in the supplied data.

## Recommendations

- Investigate cancellation and no-show patterns.
- Review Power and Internet costs.
- Introduce demand-informed pricing.
- Improve room inventory tracking.
- Monitor booking channel performance.
